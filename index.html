Qiong Wu

Qiong Wu is a Principal Inventive Scientist at AT&T Chief Data Office. Her research focuses on making machine learning systems reliable, efficient and interpretable. Current interests include large language models (reasoning, knowledge editing and efficient serving), multimodal learning and knowledge-based question answering, data-centric AI (data valuation and selection), and trustworthy ML (interpretability, out-of-distribution and anomaly detection), with a focus on building rigorous benchmarks for these settings. Her earlier work developed robust learning algorithms for high-dimensional, low signal-to-noise problems, with applications in finance and mobile sensing.

Before joining AT&T, Qiong obtained her Ph.D. in computer science at William & Mary. She received her B.Eng degree from the Dalian University of Technology and M.Sc degree from the University of Hong Kong.
